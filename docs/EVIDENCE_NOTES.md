# Evidence notes for phases 3-5 (verified Oct 2026)

- Rest: Schoenfeld 2016 JSCR (3 min > 1 min, trained men; doi 10.1519/JSC.0000000000001267 via PMID 26605807). Singer 2024 Front Sports Act Living 6:1429789 (doi 10.3389/fspor.2024.1429789): small benefit >60 s, no clear difference beyond 90 s. Senna 2011 JSCR: compounds lose more reps with short rest. App: compounds 2-3 min, isolation 90-120 s (inference, labelled), adjustable.
- Failure, trained: Refalo 2024 J Sports Sci 42(1):85-101 doi 10.1080/02640414.2024.2321021 (failure vs 1-2 RIR identical quad growth). Robinson 2024 Sports Med doi 10.1007/s40279-024-02069-2 (hypertrophy rises closer to failure; strength negligible). No study compares block start at 1-2 vs 3 RIR; ramp is convention.
- Beginners: Halperin 2022 Sports Med doi 10.1007/s40279-021-01559-x (RIR underpredicted ~1 rep; worse above 12 reps; experience did NOT improve accuracy). Refalo 2023 Sports Med doi 10.1007/s40279-022-01784-y (failure not needed, mostly untrained). Refalo 2025 Eur J Sport Sci doi 10.1002/ejsc.12266 (failure: more discomfort, worse affect). Schwartz 2021 PLoS ONE doi 10.1371/journal.pone.0256231.
- Load: Schoenfeld 2017 JSCR doi 10.1519/JSC.0000000000002200; Lopez 2021 MSSE doi 10.1249/MSS.0000000000002585 (hypertrophy same across loads; strength favours heavy).
- Deload/blocks: Bell 2023 Sports Med Open doi 10.1186/s40798-023-00633-0; Bell 2025 SCJ doi 10.1519/SSC.0000000000000910 (planned every 4-8 weeks or reactive).
- HYROX: Brandt 2025 Front Physiol doi 10.3389/fphys.2025.1519240 (VO2max, endurance volume, body fat predict time; running ~60% of time). Format: 8 x 1 km + SkiErg 1000 m, sled push 50 m, sled pull 50 m, burpee broad jumps 80 m, row 1000 m, farmers carry 200 m, sandbag lunges 100 m, wall balls 100 (hyrox.com/the-fitness-race/).
- CrossFit: Claudino 2018 Sports Med Open doi 10.1186/s40798-018-0124-5 (no significant outcome effects, low quality). Hülsmann 2021 Dtsch Z Sportmed doi 10.5960/dzsm.2021.504 (3.2 injuries/1000 h; spine, shoulder, knee).
- Triathlon: Muñoz 2014 IJSPP doi 10.1123/IJSPP.2012-0352; Neal 2011 J Sports Sci doi 10.1080/02640414.2011.596217 (~70% easy). Weich 2022 Front Sports Act Living doi 10.3389/fspor.2022.1065741 (heavy legs after bike). Taper Bosquet 2007, Wang 2023 PLoS ONE doi 10.1371/journal.pone.0282838.
- Beginner running: NHS Couch to 5K, 9 weeks, 3 runs/week (nhs.uk). Riegel 1981 American Scientist 69(3):285-290: t2 = t1 (d2/d1)^1.08 (NOT 1.06).
- Ultra: Knechtle & Nikolaidis 2018 Front Physiol doi 10.3389/fphys.2018.00634: any run longer than 42.195 km.
- METs: 2024 Adult Compendium (Herrmann 2024, doi 10.1016/j.jshs.2023.10.010), PDF pacompendium.com. No padel, CrossFit or HYROX entries (use paddleball, circuit training 02040 7.5, HIIT 02214 11.0).
