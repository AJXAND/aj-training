# Korfball

## Demands
- Mixed team: 8 a side, 4 women and 4 men; two zones of 4 v 4; teams switch attack and defence every two goals. Indoor court 20 m x 40 m; korf 3.5 m high, no backboard. Common format 2 x 25 min (Wikipedia summary of IKF rules).
- No dribbling or walking with the ball; one landing foot fixed; cannot shoot when defended. So play is many short sprints to lose a defender, sudden stops, jump-stops, running-in shots and rebound jumps (practice).
- No time-motion study found. Closest models: netball and basketball (high-intensity intermittent, frequent decelerations and landings).
- Energy: intermittent high intensity on an aerobic base.

## Injuries
- Alp and Özdinç 2020 (118 players): foot and ankle 34%, knee 20%, back 10%. Strains 33%, sprains 28%.
- Jans, den Bak and Backx 1998 (indoor players aged 30+): 7.2 injuries per 1,000 h; strains 45%, contusions 20%, sprains 20%; twisting 27%; 30% from contact despite rules; 67% caused time loss (median 4 weeks). Leavers had more knee injuries (42%).
- Pattern matches netball: ankle sprains most common, knee (ACL, meniscus) most costly.

## Prevention (graded)
1. `ankle` balance training: strong by meta-analysis across sports (de Vasconcelos 2018: RR 0.62).
2. `landing` (deceleration, jump-stop, cutting): strong in other jump-land sports (Webster and Hewett 2018 in Part 0).
3. `calf` and Achilles: practice (strains common, older players).
4. `patellar` tendon load management: practice/moderate.
5. `hipAbd`: moderate.
6. `nordic` for hamstring strains in sprint-heavy veterans: strong in football, practice here.

## Variants
- Zones switch, so no fixed positions. Veterans (30+): strain risk; calf and hamstring priority (practice).

## Season (UK)
- England Korfball indoor league season roughly September to April; outdoor and beach tournaments in summer (practice). Off May-July, pre August.
- Gym: off 3, pre 2, in 2.

## Tests (borrow netball and basketball bands; practice)
- 10 m sprint, 505, CMJ, Yo-Yo IR1, single-leg hop symmetry.

## Peaking
- Tournament days have several short games: no gym during; landing warm-up before each game.
