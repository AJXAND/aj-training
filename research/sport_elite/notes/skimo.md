# Ski mountaineering and ski touring: research note

## Demands
- Race formats: sprint ~3-3.5 min, vertical 20-30 min, individual 1.5-2 h, teams up to ~3 h, relay <15 min. Olympic at Milano-Cortina 2026 (sprints and mixed relay).
- Elite men VO2max ~71 ml/kg/min vs ~62.5 sub-elite. Individual races average ~93% HRmax; team races ~87%; even downhills ~85% (Bortolan 2021).
- Mostly aerobic uphill work with the skis and skins, poling (lats, triceps), transitions; eccentric quad and knee load on descents, often fatigued. Recreational touring: long days of 800-1,500 m climbing with a pack, multi-day.
- Strength: heavy strength added to endurance training improves economy and performance (Ronnestad & Mujika 2014); heavy loads (>=90% 1RM) give a small running-economy benefit, plyometrics trivial (Eihara 2022). No skimo-specific strength trials found.

## Injuries
- Survey of 104 ski mountaineers: mostly lower body, knee most common (29.6%). Injured skiers did more ski sessions and competitions and fewer core sessions per week (Etayo-Urtasun 2022). Injury incidence relatively low versus other winter sports (cited in Bortolan 2021). Avalanche and falls are the major hazards off-piste (not gym-modifiable).

## Prevention (graded)
- Knee control and landing work (strong in team sports, practice here), calf and hip abductor strength (practice), lumbar endurance for pack carrying (practice), trunk training (moderate: one association study).

## Season (UK athletes)
- Snow season Dec-Apr (mostly Alps trips). Off May-Aug: gym 3. Pre Sep-Nov: gym 2. On snow: gym 1.

## Tests
- VO2max: elite >68 (verified elite ~71, sub-elite ~62.5); good 55-65 and club <55 are practice.
- Uphill time trial on a fixed climb (self). Trap bar or squat est. 1RM x BM (practice). Single-leg ski-stance hold (self, symmetry).

## Peaking
- 7-10 days: endurance volume down 40-60%, keep intensity; last heavy lift 7 days out.
