# Shooting sports (rifle, pistol, shotgun / clay)

## Demands
- Precision under static load. Air rifle and pistol: 60 shots in qualification plus finals; smallbore 3-position adds prone and kneeling (practice).
- Rifle: heavy rifle held in standing with the leading hip pushed towards the target and spine side-bent and extended; jackets and boots restrict movement.
- Pistol: one-arm hold of the pistol at shoulder height, repeated 60+ times with long holds; shoulder and forearm endurance, wrist.
- Shotgun (trap, skeet, sporting): repeated gun mount and swing through rotation, recoil to the shoulder and cheek, 100-200 targets on big days, walking between stands (practice).
- Performance: Ihalainen 2018 (elite air rifle) found horizontal hold stability, aiming accuracy, timing and cleanness of triggering explained 81% of score variance; postural balance acted directly and through hold stability.
- Energy: low. Aerobic fitness for long days and calm heart rate (practice).

## Injuries
- Harr et al. 2021 systematic review (19 studies): acute injury prevalence low versus other sports; surveillance likely misses chronic pain.
  - Rifle: low back most common. Pistol: wrist and hand. Shotgun: shoulder (recoil), including a coracoid fracture in a trap shooter.
- Urbach et al. 2025: 89% of 114 college rifle athletes had low back pain linked to shooting; 99% of women vs 74% of men; 24% missed training or competition; pain on about 45% of season days.
- Others: neck pain (head tilt to sight), noise-induced hearing loss (protection is mandatory), shoulder bruising from poor gun fit (practice).

## Prevention (graded)
1. `lowback` trunk endurance (extensors, side bridge) and hip mobility: moderate (Urbach 2025 recommends core and position-specific work; no trial).
2. `trunkLat` lateral trunk for the side-bent standing position: practice.
3. `shoulderER` and `scap` for pistol hold arm and shotgun mount: practice.
4. `wrist` for pistol: practice.
5. `tspine` and `neck` mobility for sighting posture: practice.
6. Balance training: postural balance links to score (Ihalainen 2018); balance work is low risk (practice for score gains).

## Variants
- Rifle: lumbar and lateral trunk priority.
- Pistol: hold-arm shoulder and wrist endurance, both sides trained.
- Shotgun: rotation, recoil tolerance, gun mount speed, legs for walking stands.

## Season (UK)
- Target rifle and pistol: indoor winter leagues (Oct-Mar), outdoor fullbore and smallbore summer (Apr-Sep). Clay shooting all year with summer peak (practice).
- Gym: off 3, pre 2, in 2.

## Tests (bands practice; no sport norms)
- Side plank hold each side.
- Back extension hold (Biering-Sorensen style) on a bench.
- Single-leg balance eyes closed.
- Shoulder ER:IR ratio (Part 0).

## Peaking
- Last hard gym 72 h before; light mobility and balance only in the final 2 days. Avoid shoulder fatigue the day before.
