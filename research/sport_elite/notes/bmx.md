# BMX: research note

## Demands
- Racing: one lap, 8 riders, 300-400 m with a start hill, jumps and berms. Elite men about 39.7 s, women about 41 s (2010 Worlds). Men pedal ~31% of the time, jump ~25%, pump ~44%; ~30 pedal strokes (Cowell 2011). Peak power and cadence within ~6 m and 1.6 s of the gate (Herman 2009, cited by Cowell).
- Peak power 1,285-1,498 W in elite riders; ~89% of race time below 50% of peak power; VO2 reaches ~94% of VO2max and lactate ~14.5 mmol/L in simulated racing (Becerra-Patino 2025 systematic review, 21 studies, small samples).
- Strength links: 1RM strength r = 0.84 and repeated-sprint peak power r = 0.87 with performance; jump height r about -0.80 with race time (same review). The authors of the time-motion study call BMX a strength and power sport; Wingate tests largely irrelevant.
- Freestyle (park, dirt, street, flatland): runs of about a minute, big airs and landings. Almost no physiology data.

## Injuries
- Contusions, lacerations, abrasions and fractures most common; collarbone/shoulder, wrist, head (concussion) from crashes. Injury rates 37.5 per 100 athletes at Rio 2016, 11.7 at 2007 Worlds, 6.6 at 1989 Europeans; one study: freestyle 22.2, racing 27.1 per 100 (Sports Health 2025 scoping review, 37 studies).
- Risk factors: age, sex, riders per race, prior injury, bike characteristics. Prevention almost untested; fewer riders per race may help; neck brace reduced head rotational acceleration in one lab study only.
- British Cycling 2011-13: BMX squad had the highest injury prevalence and severity of all GB squads (Palmer-Green 2014 abstract).

## Prevention (graded)
- No BMX-specific trials. Use: shoulder ER and scapular work (practice), wrist strength (practice), neck isometrics (moderate, from collision sports), landing control for freestyle (strong in team sports, practice here), helmet and gloves, concussion protocol.

## Season (UK amateur)
- Racing roughly spring to autumn with regional and national series; winter is the main strength block. Off 3 gym, pre 2, in 2 (power sport, short races).

## Tests (coach practice unless stated)
- CMJ: club <40, good 40-50, elite >50 cm.
- Squat est. 1RM: <1.5, 1.5-1.8, 1.8+ x BM.
- 6 s bike peak power: elite >1,300 W (verified range 1,285-1,498 W); lower bands practice.
- Pull-ups; gate-to-first-jump time (self-comparison).

## Peaking
- 7-10 day taper: last heavy legs 5-7 days out, volume halved, gate starts kept.
