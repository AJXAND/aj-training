# Diving: research note

## Demands
- Springboard (1 m, 3 m), platform (5, 7.5, 10 m), synchro, and high diving (20 m women, 27 m men, feet-first entry).
- Each dive: one maximal take-off (or armstand), 1-3 s of flight with fast rotation, then entry. ATP-PC dominant; low aerobic demand, many repeats per session.
- Take-off height, rotation speed and tight shapes need leg power, trunk stiffness, hip flexor/compression strength and flexibility. No good diver-specific strength-performance studies found; transfer from gymnastics and jumps (practice).

## Injuries
- Systematic review (28 studies, pre-elite and elite): shoulder, lower back, trunk and wrist most affected. Competition incidence 1.9-15.5 per 1,000 exposures. Low back pain up to 89% lifetime, 37% point prevalence. Wrist pain point prevalence 86% in 10 m platform divers (one 1993 study). Shoulder 22.7% of injuries at 2013 Worlds. Water entry is the mechanism in ~71% of reported cases; entry speeds ~60 km/h. Platform: more upper limb; springboard: more lower limb (limited evidence). Illness: respiratory most common (Currie 2025).
- Older review: back and reverse dives and platform carry more injuries; wrist, shoulder and lumbar spine most injured (Clinics in Sports Medicine 1999).

## Prevention (graded)
- No diving prevention trials. Lumbar endurance and anti-extension trunk (practice), shoulder ER/scapular (moderate in overhead sports), wrist loading (practice), patellar tendon care for springboard (practice), landing control for high divers (practice). Youth: follow youth resistance-training consensus; extension back pain in a young diver = medical review.

## Season
- Competitions spread through the year; plan 6-10 week builds before target meets. Off 3, pre 2, in 2 short sessions; avoid all heavy work the day before competition.

## Tests (coach practice)
- CMJ with arm swing (self-comparison; no diver norms found).
- Squat est. 1RM: <1.25, 1.25-1.5, 1.5+ x BM (borrowed from gymnastics).
- Handstand hold 60 s+ (platform). Pull-ups. Pike sit-and-reach (self).

## Peaking
- Last heavy session 7-10 days out; volume halved; no maxing within 10 days.
