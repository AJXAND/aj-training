# Polo (field and arena)

## Demands
- Chukkas of 7 minutes; 3 minutes to change ponies between chukkas; 5 min half-time (Ham Polo Club rules page, HPA rules). Low and medium goal matches are usually 4 chukkas (practice).
- Elite match heart rate 150 +/- 20 bpm, highest in the 3rd chukka (154 bpm); most playing time in the "hard" zone (Wright and Peters 2008). The rider is working hard, not just sitting.
- Riding off (hip to hip contact at speed), hooking, two-point stance in stirrups, leaning out of the saddle to strike: big rotational, adductor, trunk and hitting-arm demand.
- Hitting arm: mallet swing with wrist and forearm; grip on reins with the other hand.
- Energy: repeated high-intensity efforts in 7-min blocks on an aerobic base.

## Injuries
- Costa-Paz et al. 1999 (Argentine high goal, 34 riders): 7.8 injuries per 1,000 player-game hours. Arms 39%, legs 31%, head 19%. Fractures 39%, mostly from falls.
- Neumayr and Schmitt 2019 (103 riders, Germany and Switzerland): 40% injured in 2 years, 67% of injuries were major (> 30 days). Tournament rates 5.3 (pro) and 6.1 (amateur) per 1,000 h. Shoulder 20%, distal forearm 9%, spine 13%, head 11%, concussion 7%. Falls from the horse 44%. Overuse pain in 55%: wrist 21%, low back 21%; typical pattern lumbar spine, groin and hitting arm.
- Only 7% wore helmets meeting current standards in that sample; authors recommend certified helmets and face protection.
- HPA (UK) adopted the 2023 UK grassroots concussion guidance: no return before 21 days after concussion or suspected concussion.

## Prevention (graded)
1. `lowback` trunk endurance and hip extension: practice (21% overuse pain).
2. `copenhagen` adductor capacity (gripping the horse, groin pain pattern): practice in polo, strong in football.
3. `wrist` and forearm on the hitting arm: practice.
4. `shoulderER` rotator cuff (swing and falls): practice.
5. `neck` strength for falls: practice (moderate in collision sports).
6. `trunkLat` rotation control: practice.
- Equipment: certified helmet and face protection. Fall technique practice.

## Variants
- Field polo: 4-a-side, big field, high speed, long swings.
- Arena polo: 3-a-side, enclosed, smaller ball, more riding off and short-stick work, usually winter (practice).
- Back (No. 4): more defensive riding off and backhands (practice).

## Season (UK)
- Outdoor: May to September. Arena: October to April. Off-horse gym: off 3 (winter if not playing arena), pre 2 (Mar-Apr), in 2.

## Tests (bands practice)
- Adductor squeeze or Copenhagen hold time.
- Side plank.
- Rotational med ball throw (track change, both sides).
- Two-point position wall sit (s).
- Back extension hold.

## Peaking
- Big tournament: last heavy lower 4 days out; keep 1 short session; plenty of stick and ball.
